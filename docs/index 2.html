Experimental Design & Analysis in Biology

BIOL 4860/5860 — University of Massachusetts Lowell — Spring 2027

Welcome

Experiments are one of the main ways we expand our understanding of biology. In this hands-on course, you’ll develop fundamental skills in experimental design, data analysis, and visualization — and learn to use R to analyze and visualize data. By the end, you’ll be able to describe experimental designs and analytic techniques (t-tests, ANOVA, linear regression, mixed-effects models, and more) commonly used in biological research, evaluate the appropriateness of different designs, and work through analytic workflows of medium complexity.
